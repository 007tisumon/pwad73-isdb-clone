Welcome to IsDB-BISEW

The Islamic Development Bank-Bangladesh Islamic Solidarity Educational Wakf (IsDB-BISEW) was established in 1987 following an agreement between the Islamic Development Bank, Jeddah, Saudi Arabia and the Government of Bangladesh. IsDB-BISEW undertakes funding, formulating and implementing of programmes in the areas of education, developing human resources and strengthening educational institutions.
